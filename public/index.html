A hiring pipeline any recruiting tool can plug into.

Meridian Hire is an API for recruiting teams. Companies post jobs, candidates apply with a résumé, their GitHub and the projects they've built, and each application moves from Applied to Hired, with every step on record. This board is a window onto it.

Try the API in your browser

Creates a private sandbox company with made-up candidates. It is yours alone and is deleted after 24 hours.

  • Each company is walled offThe database itself refuses to show one company another's records.
  • Every move is on recordStage changes are saved with who made them and why, and can't be edited later.
  • Other systems hear about itEach change is sent, signed, to the firm's own software, and retried if it doesn't arrive.
  • Candidates can be erasedOne request removes a person, their applications and their files, for privacy requests.

What it becomes for your firm

The demo runs on the real engine. These parts aren't in it yet: with a pilot or a custom build, Meridian Interface adds the ones your firm needs and leaves out the ones it doesn't.

  • Your team signs inA login for each recruiter, with roles that decide who sees offers and personal data.
  • Your careers pageYour jobs and an application form in your branding, feeding this pipeline.
  • Emails and schedulingCandidate emails for each stage, and interview times picked from Google or Outlook calendars.
  • Feedback and reportsScorecards from each interviewer, time to hire, and where your best candidates come from.
  • Connections to your toolsThe same signed updates, wired to Slack, your CRM or your HR system.
  • Agency modeClient companies, submissions and placements, for staffing firms.
